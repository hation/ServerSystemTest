FirPE Driver Restore
====================

The ps3stor driver (Linkdata 2230-18i) is restored here.

Copy the "FirPE" folder to your USB drive root so it becomes:
  D:\FirPE\Drivers\ps3stor.inf
  D:\FirPE\Drivers\ps3stor.sys
  D:\FirPE\Drivers\ps3stor.cat

(Replace D: with your USB drive letter.)

FirPE auto-scans U-disk FirPE\Drivers on boot and loads the driver.
Then run FirpeCleaner64.exe from the USB drive (NOT the FirPE desktop -
the desktop is a RAM disk X: and is wiped on reboot).
